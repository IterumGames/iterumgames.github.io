Tumbler Cups

A one-tap sorting puzzle for Android.

Now in closed testing on Google Play. Want to play it early? Join the closed test: it takes two minutes, through our testing group.

Tap the pieces at the top of a tumbling pile. Each one drops into a cup of its colour, or into a small holding tray. Fill a cup to clear it, and empty the pile to rescue the creature trapped underneath. Every level is checked to be winnable without help.

How the game makes money

  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one for a bonus.
  • An optional Supporter Pack and tips, which never make the game easier.
  • No lives, no timers, no paywalls, no forced ads.
