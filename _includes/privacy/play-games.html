Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games on your phone, the game signs you in to it automatically{% if page.play_games.setting %} (you can also sign in from {{ page.play_games.setting }}){% endif %}. Google then shares your Play Games name and player ID with the game so it can show them, and the game tells Google when you unlock {{ page.play_games.features | default: "an achievement" }}.

  • Who receives it: Google, through Google Play Games Services, under the Google privacy policy. We don't receive or store any of it. Google also collects technical and usage data to keep Play Games working.
  • Your choices: in the Google Play Games app you choose who can see your profile and game activity, can turn off automatic sign-in, and can delete your Play Games data.
