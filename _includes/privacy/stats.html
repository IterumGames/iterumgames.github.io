Gameplay stats{% unless page.diagnostics or page.crash_reports %} and crash reports{% endunless %} (opt-in)

When you first play, the game asks whether you want to share gameplay stats. If you say no, {% if page.diagnostics or page.crash_reports %}no gameplay stats are sent{% else %}nothing is sent{% endif %} and none of the services below is started for stats. If you say yes:

  • What is sent: game events such as {{ page.stats.events }}{% if page.diagnostics or page.crash_reports %}, and a short note of any error the game ran into{% else %}; plus crash and error reports{% endif %}. They come with a random ID created for this install, your device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country (from your IP address).
  • What is never sent for stats: your name, email, phone number, precise location or your advertising ID.
  • Who receives it: Unity Gaming Services ({% if page.diagnostics or page.crash_reports %}Analytics{% else %}Analytics and crash diagnostics{% endif %}) and Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Why: {{ page.stats.purpose }}. Our legal basis is your consent.
  • How long: Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months. Unity keeps it for as long as its standard retention period allows, or until you ask for deletion.

You can change your choice at any time in {{ page.stats.setting }}. {% if page.stats.delete %}{{ page.stats.delete }} turns sharing off, asks Unity to delete the data linked to your ID, and resets the Google Analytics ID on your device.{% else %}Turning it off stops collection straight away. To ask for data you already sent to be deleted, email us.{% endif %}
