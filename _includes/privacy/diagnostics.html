Crash, error and performance reports (always sent)

The game uses Unity's built-in diagnostics so we can find and fix crashes, errors, freezes and slow performance. This runs for every player. It doesn't depend on your answer to the stats question, and the game has no switch for it.

  • What is sent: when the game crashes, hits an error or stops responding, a technical report (the kind of problem and where in the game's code it happened); and routine performance measurements such as start-up time, frame rate and memory use. They come with a random ID created for this install, your device model, operating system version, app version and approximate country (from your IP address).
  • What is never sent: your name, anything you type, anything from your saved games, your precise location or your advertising ID. The game is set never to attach its log to a report.
  • Who receives it: Unity, which processes it for us.
  • Why: to fix problems and keep the game running well. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in a game that works.
  • How long: Unity keeps it for up to 90 days.

To ask about this data or request its deletion, email us.
