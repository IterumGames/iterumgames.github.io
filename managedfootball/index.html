Managed Football

A football club management game for Android.

You are the manager and the chief executive of a club in an invented world, where every club, player and competition is made up. Pick the starting eleven, set the tactics, run training and work the transfer market, then watch the match play out on an animated pitch. Off the pitch, rebuild the ground stand by stand, add facilities, sign sponsors and keep the accounts healthy enough to satisfy the board. It plays offline in portrait, with no account needed.

How the game makes money

  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one for a cosmetic look.
  • An optional Supporter Pack and tips, which never make the game easier.
  • No timers, no paywalls, no forced ads.

Play with your own clubs

You can start a career from a database you write yourself. How to make and import a database, with an example template to download.
