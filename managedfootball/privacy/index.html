Deleting careers and custom databases

To delete one career, open Load career and tap the cross next to it. To delete a custom database, open it under New career › Custom database and tap Remove this file from the folder. Uninstalling the game deletes everything, including your settings and club styles.

Custom databases you import

If you start a career with your own database, the game reads the file you pick on your phone (or copy into its Databases folder) and keeps a copy in its own storage. The file is read only on your phone and is never uploaded. Apart from the stats and ads described above, the game doesn't send or download anything: it plays offline and needs no account. (Settings › Privacy policy › Open the online version opens this page in your phone's browser.)
